What this is
Novel Voices brings music education to students in Kenya, Palestine, and Iraq. The program is moving every enrolled student onto one cohort curriculum — no more one-off individual lessons. From that shared base, tiers layer on more: Promising Students get the full 2-year cohort track, and the 1–2 Intensive Students per site get the most — conservatory-focused study, possibly with financial aid.
Immediate direction — one-off lessons phasing out
By the end of the 2026–27 year, every site moves to cohort-only. One-off, individual lessons that aren't part of a cohort are being phased out everywhere except Don Speer's piano studio, which continues regardless.

What this tier is — where we're headed
The base of the pyramid is every enrolled student on the shared 2-year cohort curriculum. One-off, individual lessons outside a cohort are being phased out — the only exception is Don Speer's piano studio, which continues regardless. From this shared base, Promising Students get more, and Intensive Students get the most.
Phase-out timeline, by site
  • Kenya: one-off lessons continue only through the 2026–27 year, then stop — except Don Speer's piano studio
  • Palestine: one-off lessons wind down as the new Year 1 cohort pilot ramps up this year
  • Iraq: paused. The team is restructuring and plans to stay in touch about when and how the cohort model arrives there
The 2-year cohort package
  • 8 students per site (cushion for 2 more — 10 total if absolutely necessary)
  • Local partner space with high-speed internet (already in the works in Kenya)
  • Materials provided: instruments, tablets, luthier tools (instruments and some luthier tools already in place in Kenya)
  • Transportation provided (already handled in Kenya, managed by the field coordinator)
  • Snacks provided (already covered in Kenya by the field coordinator)

Promising Students

What this tier is
Within the cohort, Promising Students are the ones showing real potential for higher-education opportunities down the line. They get more than the base cohort curriculum — extra attention, extra resources — without yet being the 1–2 conservatory-track Intensive Students.
ABRSM — the clearest marker
Promising Students are likely the cut-off group we actively encourage to sit ABRSM exams — and PMHU covers the testing fees for them. Progress toward ABRSM levels (Year 1 students are already tracking toward Level 8 by year 2) is a natural signal for identifying who belongs in this tier.
What else likely comes with it
  • Extra one-on-one coaching time with lead teachers, beyond the standard weekly lesson slot
  • Priority placement in visiting-artist masterclasses and workshops during festival visits
  • First look at solo repertoire spots in cohort concerts
  • Additional practice materials or method books beyond the standard kit
  • Closer, more frequent progress tracking — teachers flagging students on pace for Promising status each term
  • Optional summer bridge sessions, where staffing allows, to keep momentum between cohort years

What this tier is
The 1–2 students per site with real conservatory potential. They move beyond the extra coaching Promising Students get into individualized, one-on-one support aimed squarely at getting them into a U.S. higher-education music program.
1:1 support toward U.S. higher ed
  • Individualized audition-repertoire planning with lead teachers
  • Guidance through the U.S. conservatory application process — recordings, essays, deadlines
  • Mock auditions and interview prep
  • Direct connections to conservatory faculty and alumni where possible
  • Support navigating visas and international-student logistics
Coming eventually — a dedicated Student Support Coordinator
A Student Support Coordinator role is planned specifically for this tier — someone who builds out scholarship information and resources for international students moving toward U.S. higher ed, and helps students navigate financial aid, visas, and the parts of the application process a teacher alone can't cover.
Financial aid
Top students in this tier may be eligible for direct financial aid from PMHU, evaluated case-by-case, alongside the scholarship search the future Coordinator will support.
International Music Sessions (IMS)
A 14-day in-person intensive in the USA — still eventual. Possible relaunch in August 2029. Who gets pulled in, and from which sites, is still open.
